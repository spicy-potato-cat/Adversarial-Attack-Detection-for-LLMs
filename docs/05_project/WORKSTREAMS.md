# Workstreams

**Project:** Adversarial Attack Detection for Large Language Models (LLMs)
**Status:** ACTIVE

## 1. Documentation and governance

Responsible for naming, scope boundaries, decision recording, and repository discipline.

## 2. Data acquisition and forensic evidence

Responsible for source preservation, identity resolution, and lineage analysis under strict governance boundaries.

## 3. Research framing and hypothesis tracking

Responsible for research questions, novelty analysis, metrics, threat model, and formal research posture.

## 4. Architecture investigation

Responsible for system-level design, detector interfaces, data model, and security posture.

## 5. Experimental design and evaluation

Responsible for protocol design, ablations, reproducibility, and consensus-evasion planning.

## 6. Project status and roadmap management

Responsible for milestones, open questions, and transition points to Commander review.

## Status note

These workstreams are active conceptual groups. They do not substitute for final project approval or a finalized framework.
