# Open Questions

**Project:** Adversarial Attack Detection for Large Language Models (LLMs)
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Open governance questions

- Which datasets are stable enough to support formal evidence review?
- What evidence is required before a benchmark may be treated as a candidate for experimental use?
- Which project artifacts are subject to final approval vs. provisional research exploration?

## Open research questions

- How often do multiple detectors agree on a false benign decision?
- Can disagreement-aware routing reduce harming false negatives at manageable cost?
- Which benchmark characteristics create the strongest contamination risk?

## Open architecture questions

- What is the minimum acceptable detector contract for a heterogeneous system?
- When should a system abstain or escalate instead of producing a direct classification?
- How should false agreement be represented in risk scoring and audit logs?

## Status note

Open questions remain active and unresolved. The project records them explicitly rather than assuming an answer.
