# Hypotheses

**Project:** Adversarial Attack Detection for Large Language Models (LLMs)
**Status:** UNDER INVESTIGATION

## H1: Correlated blind spots exist

Multiple heterogeneous detectors may share correlated blind spots for adversarial prompts. A unanimous benign assessment may therefore remain incorrect even when detectors appear to agree.

## H2: False agreement has operational value

False agreement and disagreement patterns can provide useful information for conditional verification and adaptive detection workflows when evaluated under realistic cost constraints.

## H3: Evidence quality governs reliability

Dataset provenance, benchmark contamination, and overlap structure materially affect the validity of any detector comparison. Without careful accounting, apparent gains may reflect leakage or composition artifacts.

## H4: Verification routing is a tradeoff

Conditional verification may reduce unsafe misclassifications, but the value depends on false-positive rate, latency budget, and the cost of expert or model-based review.

## Status note

These hypotheses are documented as research directions only. They are not treated as established conclusions.
