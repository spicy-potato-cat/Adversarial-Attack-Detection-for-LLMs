# Retention and Privacy

**Project:** Adversarial Attack Detection for Large Language Models (LLMs)
**Status:** UNDER INVESTIGATION

## Governing principle

The repository’s governance posture requires preserving raw artifacts in immutable form and separating them from derived analysis. The privacy and retention policy therefore begins with careful evidence preservation rather than experimental use.

## Retention policy

- Preserve raw sources as immutable acquisition artifacts.
- Record hash and size metadata for all imported or manually acquired payloads.
- Store derived forensic and analysis outputs separately from raw artifacts.
- Keep lineage, review notes, and rights evidence with the dataset inventory and analysis record.

## Privacy and rights posture

- Treat privacy and legal review as mandatory before downstream reuse.
- Record uncertainty explicitly rather than assuming valid rights or clean consent.
- Mark unresolved cases with states such as `license_review`, `provenance_review`, and `pii_review` when appropriate.

## Status note

This policy is intentionally conservative. It is not a final legal clearance statement and should not be used to infer that a dataset is safe for downstream training or evaluation.
